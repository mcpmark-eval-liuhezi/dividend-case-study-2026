## 2025 Dividend Comparison

| Ticker | Company | Calendar-2025 dividends per share | Current price | Indicated yield |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| KO | The Coca-Cola Company | $2.04 | $92.71 | 2.29% |
| JNJ | Johnson & Johnson | $5.14 | $263.81 | 2.03% |

| PEP | PepsiCo, Inc. | $5.624 | $135.77 | 4.36% |

**Highest calendar-2025 payout: PepsiCo (PEP), with $5.624 per share** — ahead of Johnson & Johnson ($5.14),
Procter & Gamble ($4.178), and Coca-Cola ($2.04). PEP also carries the highest indicated yield of the group
(4.36% at $135.77), roughly double KO's 2.29% and JNJ's 2.03%. Note that all four names raised their quarterly
dividend during 2025 (KO from $0.51 to $0.53, JNJ from $1.30 to $1.34, PG from $1.057 to $1.089, PEP from $1.423
to $1.48), and all four are long-tenured dividend growers — though PEP is the only one of the four that has not
split its stock since 2000.
